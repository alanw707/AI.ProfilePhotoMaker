# 14: Prepare professional summaries and free document exports

## Parent

https://github.com/alanw707/AI.ProfilePhotoMaker/issues/376

## What to build

The user derives an editable professional summary and exports accepted materials as useful PDF/DOCX documents without buying photos or retaining model quota.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Draft factual bio/summary from the same accepted source facts and review/version workflow; no auto-publishing to LinkedIn or other sites.
- [ ] Export a selected accepted resume version to text-readable PDF and DOCX with stable layout, headings and links.
- [ ] Basic export remains free and available at generation-quota exhaustion; avoid an unsupported ATS compatibility guarantee.
- [ ] Serve private owner-authorized downloads with appropriate expiration; export IDs do not expose another user's content.
- [ ] Omit headshot by default; include photo only in a separate explicit user-selected output where supported.
- [ ] Verify rendered short/long/multilingual-name/long-link documents, extracted text order, page breaks, download privacy and quota-exhausted export.

## Blocked by

- https://github.com/alanw707/AI.ProfilePhotoMaker/issues/389 — Draft and edit a fact-grounded targeted resume

## Delivery boundary

Keep this slice demoable or independently verifiable. Extend existing API/browser testing seams and preserve unrelated work. Use synthetic fixtures, not reference-screenshot personal data. Every private entity must participate in ownership, export/deletion and retention rules. No production rollout or paid service purchase is authorized by this issue alone.
