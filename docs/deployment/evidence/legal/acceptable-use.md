## 1. Summary
Use the service only for lawful purposes. You are responsible for the content you upload and generate.

## 2. Prohibited Content
- Content you do not own or have permission to use.
- Impersonation or misrepresentation of identity.
- Illegal, abusive, or hateful content.
- Explicit sexual content or non-consensual imagery.
- Content that violates privacy or confidentiality.

## 3. Prohibited Behavior
- Attempting to bypass security or access restrictions.
- Using the service for harassment, deception, or fraud.
- Automated scraping or usage that harms service stability.

## 4. Enforcement
We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy or our Terms.
