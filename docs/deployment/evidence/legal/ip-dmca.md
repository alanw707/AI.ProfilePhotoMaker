## 1. Copyright and IP
You may only upload content that you own or have permission to use. We respect intellectual property rights.

## 2. Takedown Requests
If you believe content infringes your rights, send:
- Your contact information.
- Description of the copyrighted work.
- The URL or location of the allegedly infringing content.
- A statement of good-faith belief that use is not authorized.
- A statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate.

## 3. Counter-Notice
If you believe a takedown was submitted in error, you may send a counter-notice with sufficient details and a statement consenting to jurisdiction where applicable.

## 4. Contact
Send IP/DMCA requests to `legal@aiprofilephotomaker.com`.
