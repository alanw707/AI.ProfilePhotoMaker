# Position product around profile-photo workflow outcomes instead of headshot volume

Status: accepted

Most AI headshot competitors sell speed and output volume: upload several photos and receive dozens or hundreds of generated headshots. We will instead position AI.ProfilePhotoMaker around helping an individual professional get their best usable profile photo package: score the current photo, generate a small candidate set, pick the best shot, refine or augment it, and export platform-ready assets. Credits remain an internal ledger, Replicate custom model training remains a hidden legacy/fallback capability, and creative style packs remain a friendly secondary path rather than the main product promise.
