# Make the career agent the primary product on the existing domain

Status: accepted direction for planned implementation; supersedes ADR-0001's photo-first positioning when the pivot ships.

Keep AI Profile Photo Maker and aiprofilephotomaker.com while directly pivoting toward an agent-led career workspace for experienced U.S. professionals. The free core accepts resumes and professional background, provides sourced national market and compensation analysis, and produces career guidance and materials; profile photos remain an optional paid capability with existing fulfillment rights preserved. This trades the immediate category clarity of a new career brand for continuity and no new domain, with an explicit career-assistant descriptor explaining the broader offer.

The agent performs research and work inside the application; external submissions, communications and spending require user review. Financial portfolios, trading, wealth management and personal net-worth calculations are outside scope. Additional career pricing is deferred pending usage and cost evidence. Current photo provider and server-side purchase-delivery decisions continue to govern the photo capability.
