# LinkedIn Carousel Cadence — Feature Spec

## Overview
Build a lightweight, repeatable content system for LinkedIn carousel posts so AI Profile Photo Maker can publish 3 high-quality carousels per week without scrambling for ideas, copy, or design direction.

This is a marketing operations feature, not a product code feature. The deliverable is a documented operating system for carousel ideation, production, review, publishing, and performance tracking.

## Goal
Create a simple weekly system that makes it realistic to publish **3 LinkedIn carousels per week** consistently.

## Why this matters
- Organic LinkedIn is one of the cheapest distribution channels available right now
- Carousel posts can package product education, before/after proof, and career pain points into a format that gets saves and shares
- A cadence only works if topic selection, copywriting, asset creation, and posting are systematized

## Success Criteria
- A new carousel can be outlined in under 15 minutes
- A carousel draft can be produced in under 60 minutes
- At least 3 carousels per week can be scheduled or posted
- Every carousel has a clear CTA tied to AI Profile Photo Maker
- Performance is logged weekly so the team can learn what hooks and themes work

## Weekly Cadence
- **Monday:** Career/job-search angle
- **Wednesday:** Before/after or product education angle
- **Friday:** Founder insight, social proof, or objection-handling angle

If a week slips, minimum viable cadence is 2 posts, but target remains 3.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Job Search / Career Pain
Use when targeting professionals who need a stronger LinkedIn presence.

Examples:
- Your LinkedIn photo is a trust signal, whether people admit it or not
- 5 signs your profile photo is quietly hurting your job search
- You do not need to spend $400 on headshots anymore
- Why recruiters judge your profile in seconds

### 2. Before / After Transformation
Use when showcasing visual proof and product value.

Examples:
- Webcam selfie to professional headshot
- 5 selfies in, polished headshots out
- Real examples of bad lighting vs polished results
- From casual photo to LinkedIn-ready profile image

### 3. Objection Handling
Use when addressing common reasons people do not buy.

Examples:
- “AI headshots look fake”
- “I do not have enough photos”
- “I do not want to pay photographer prices”
- “I’m not photogenic”

### 4. Founder / Build-in-Public
Use when sharing product or growth learnings.

Examples:
- We cut the minimum upload requirement from 10 to 5, here’s why
- What we learned from watching users drop off at onboarding
- The highest-friction step in AI headshot onboarding
- Distribution > feature creep: what actually moved the needle

### 5. Social Proof / Testimonials
Use when credibility is needed.

Examples:
- What users say after updating their LinkedIn photo
- Why people use AI headshots instead of booking a studio
- Mini case study: better profile, better first impression

## Recommended Carousel Structure
Target: **6 to 8 slides** max.

### Slide 1 — Hook
Short, punchy, scroll-stopping.

Examples:
- 5 signs your LinkedIn photo is costing you opportunities
- You do not need a $400 photographer for a professional headshot
- We cut our selfie requirement in half, and it changed everything

### Slide 2 — Problem
Define the pain clearly.

### Slide 3 — Why it matters
Explain job-search, trust, or profile-conversion impact.

### Slide 4 — Shift in thinking
Challenge the old assumption.

### Slide 5 — Solution
Introduce the better path.

### Slide 6 — Proof / Example
Before-after, product result, testimonial, or founder insight.

### Slide 7 — CTA
Soft CTA preferred.

Examples:
- Want me to break this down further? Follow for more
- If you need better headshots without a studio session, try AI Profile Photo Maker
- Curious what 5 selfies can turn into? Check the link in comments

## Design Guidelines
- Keep each slide focused on **one idea only**
- Use short lines, large text, and high contrast
- Prefer dark navy / white / sky-blue palette to match email and site brand
- Do not overcrowd slides with paragraphs
- Use real before/after visuals whenever possible
- Avoid stock-photo-looking slides unless they support the point

## Brand Alignment
Match existing AI Profile Photo Maker visual language:
- Background: dark navy / slate
- Card surfaces: white
- Accent: sky blue
- Voice: practical, confident, direct, not corporate

## CTA Strategy
Rotate CTAs so posts do not all feel like ads.

Approved CTA types:
1. **Engagement CTA** — comment/follow/save
2. **Educational CTA** — follow for more job-search/profile tips
3. **Product CTA** — try AI Profile Photo Maker
4. **Soft founder CTA** — sharing what we are learning building this

Recommended ratio:
- 2 out of 3 posts = soft or educational CTA
- 1 out of 3 posts = direct product CTA

## Operating Workflow

### Step 1: Pick this week’s 3 angles
Choose one from each of these buckets:
- Career pain
- Visual proof or product education
- Founder insight or objection handling

### Step 2: Draft hook options
Write 3 hooks per carousel.
Pick the strongest one.

### Step 3: Create slide outline
Draft all slides in text first before any design work.

### Step 4: Produce assets
- before/after image
- UI screenshot
- testimonial snippet
- short supporting stat or insight

### Step 5: Build carousel
Use a repeatable design template.

### Step 6: Final review
Checklist:
- clear hook
- readable slides
- no clutter
- CTA present
- product mention feels natural
- link strategy decided

### Step 7: Publish and track
Log post date, theme, hook, CTA, and results.

## Measurement Plan
Track per post:
- publish date
- topic/pillar
- hook used
- CTA type
- impressions
- likes
- comments
- reposts
- saves (if available)
- clicks/signups if attributable

Review once per week:
- best-performing hook
- best-performing pillar
- strongest CTA style
- whether carousels are driving profile visits or site traffic

## Risks
- Posting too promotional too often will tank reach
- Weak hooks will kill distribution before the value is seen
- Inconsistent visual design will make the series feel random
- No performance review means no learning loop

## Recommendations
- Build one reusable carousel design template first
- Write the carousel in plain text before opening any design tool
- Keep the first 3 carousels tightly focused on job-search pain + reduced friction + before/after proof
- Bias toward clarity and punch over cleverness
