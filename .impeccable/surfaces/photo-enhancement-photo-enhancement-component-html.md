# `/app/enhance` Surface Brief

## Scope and mode

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Scope:** Complete mobile-first professional-photo package flow from source upload through export. The visual world is new for this route; unrelated routes remain unchanged.
- **Audience and job:** An individual professional, often on a phone and between other tasks, needs to turn one source image into the exact candidate set and exports included in Free Preview, Starter, or Pro.
- **Primary task:** Advance the active package to its next fulfillment milestone. The interface always exposes one dominant next action and shows what the package still owes.

## Product truth and constraints

- Free Preview = 1 watermarked candidate and no export kit.
- Starter = 3 candidates; Pro = 9 candidates. A promoted Free Preview is paid candidate 1.
- Candidate slots, refinements, premium augmentations, and export availability are separate allowances.
- Preserve email verification, biometric consent, quality gates, retention language, and existing provider/storage boundaries.
- Do not redesign pricing checkout, package definitions, unrelated routes, or legacy training flows.

## Selected direction — Studio Proof Desk

A portrait studio presenting proofs, not an AI dashboard. Matte photo-paper fields, charcoal production ink, cobalt grease-pencil selection marks, and a restrained proof-red status stamp create the route’s visual language. Photography remains the largest material; notation explains rather than decorates.

- **Structural thesis:** The selected portrait leads; its contact sheet stays within reach; a clipped fulfillment ticket names package, progress, remaining allowances, and the one next action.
- **First mobile viewport:** Compact route header; fulfillment ticket (`Pro · 1 of 9 generated`); large selected proof; horizontal candidate strip when candidates exist; thumb-level primary action. Setup states replace the proof with the current task without introducing a competing action.
- **Desktop translation:** A two-column proofing table: selected image and contact sheet dominate the left; sticky fulfillment ticket and current task controls occupy the right. The sequence remains identical to mobile.
- **Signature interaction:** Choosing a candidate moves one cobalt proof mark and updates the recommendation/score in place. Fulfillment milestones receive one restrained status-stamp transition; no scattered hover theater.
- **Responsive principle:** Dense ticket detail folds into a four-line summary on phones and expands on demand. The primary action remains reachable and never relies on scrolling back to a previous stage.
- **Honest risk:** Professional proofing language must remain plain enough for first-time users; crop marks, stamps, and notation may never overpower faces or guidance.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** A personal proofing table makes package fulfillment visible; refuse the generic AI wizard and equal-weight settings-card grid.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Warm photo paper, charcoal ink, cobalt proof marks, restrained red stamps, matte frames, clipped job tickets, and portrait-first scale.
- **STORY:** Upload one source, pass quality, generate what the package owes, choose the strongest proof, refine deliberately, export confidently.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Package ticket above a dominant portrait; candidate proofs immediately adjacent; one sticky bottom action advances the active milestone.
- **FORM:** Studio Proof Desk, grounded direction 1 and Impeccable’s pick; seed `de8ff0f8`.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Interaction and state floor

- Show package name, generated/total candidates, remaining refinements, remaining premium augmentations, and export availability without mixing allowances.
- Block refinement/add-on emphasis until initial candidate fulfillment is complete; never label package generation as regeneration.
- Preserve upload/source context and package state across checkout return, refresh, and mobile interruption where existing APIs permit.
- Cover initial, scoring, quality warning, quality blocked, Free Preview ready, paid-upgrade return, generating, partial fulfillment, review, refining, augmentation, export, completed, expired preview, exhausted allowance, loading, and recoverable error states.
- Use semantic headings, native buttons/inputs, visible focus, live status announcements, non-color state labels, WCAG AA contrast, 44px targets, 200% zoom tolerance, and reduced-motion support.
