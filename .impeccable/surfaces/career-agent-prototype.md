# Career workspace prototype surface brief

## Scope and mode

Operate. Six connected, interactive pages for experienced U.S. professionals exploring a next role. The task is to confirm evidence, understand a market, choose a path and prepare materials. The logo and name remain AI Profile Photo Maker; the career system is separate from the existing photo editor's visual rules.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** A personal research dossier keeps the work artifact and its evidence in front; the agent is present across pages without replacing the pages.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Light research-paper field, deep ink, teal selected marks, compact source annotations, visible rules, flat working surfaces, Manrope headings and DM Sans UI text. The left rail is the contents, the center is the evidence, the right panel is contextual help.
- **STORY:** A professional confirms background, inspects the range definition and missing evidence, compares markets, chooses a roadmap, edits materials and optionally prepares photos.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Current goal above a strong next-action brief in the center; six labeled destinations on the left; right assistant reads the active artifact. A phone sees the same work before the optional assistant sheet.
- **FORM:** Grounded candidate 3, a career research dossier, from Impeccable direction seed `714cda7d`; code-led implementation. Signature interaction: accepting a factual correction visibly marks dependent analysis stale across the six-page journey.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Content and constraints

All people and monetary values are fictional and labeled. The source qualification has not approved personalized advertised-pay estimates, so the prototype shows that result as unavailable. No screenshot user data, mass application, financial portfolio or photo-based career scoring. Photo entry is optional. The research and materials paths remain usable without buying photos.
