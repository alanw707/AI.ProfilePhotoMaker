# Social Quick-Win Campaign Plan

**Product:** AI Profile Photo Maker
**Goal:** Drive immediate traffic and conversions via social in 2 weeks
**Core Promise:** Studio-quality headshots at a low cost, delivered fast

## Goals and KPIs

- Primary: Paid conversions from social traffic
- Secondary: Click-through rate (CTR) and landing page sessions
- Benchmarks to watch:
  - CTR: 0.8%+ (LinkedIn), 1.0%+ (IG/FB), 1.2%+ (X)
  - Conversion rate: 2%+ to paid (or 5%+ to signup if that is the conversion event)
- Baseline rule: use the first 3 days as baseline, then aim for +20-30% CTR/CR lift by day 7.

## Audience and Positioning

### Primary Audience
- Job seekers and professionals updating LinkedIn or resumes

### Secondary Audience
- Freelancers, founders, remote workers, consultants

### Positioning
- Benefit-forward: "Studio-quality headshots in minutes, at a fraction of the cost."
- Trust: "Looks like you, just more professional."
- Speed: "Fast turnaround, no studio visit."

### Objections to Address
- "Will it look fake?" -> Emphasize natural, realistic look
- "Is it expensive?" -> Emphasize low cost compared to studio
- "Will I need a photoshoot?" -> Emphasize upload + quick results

## Offer and CTA

- Primary CTA: "Get your headshots in minutes"
- Secondary CTAs:
  - "Upgrade your LinkedIn photo today"
  - "See pricing and styles"
  - "Try it now"

## Landing Page Decision

- Default landing page: `https://app.aiprofilephotomaker.com/pricing`
- Test fallback: if CTR is fine but CR is weak after day 3, test homepage for 3 days.

## Channel Strategy

- LinkedIn: Professional benefit + proof
- Instagram: Visual proof + before/after + story format
- Facebook: Trust + testimonials + simple CTA
- X: Short, fast benefits + link

## Paid Boost Plan (Low Budget)

- Budget: $10-$25/day for 7-14 days
- Allocation: 60% Instagram, 30% LinkedIn, 10% Facebook/X
- Objective: Conversions if pixel/Insight Tag is active; otherwise website traffic.

### Targeting Suggestions
- LinkedIn: Job seekers, marketing, engineering, sales, recruiters, "open to work"
- Instagram/Facebook: Interests in LinkedIn, job search, resumes, career growth, startups
- Location: English-speaking geos first (US/UK/AU/CA) unless you have a known focus

## Tracking and Reporting

- Use UTM params for every link:
  - utm_source: linkedin | instagram | facebook | x
  - utm_medium: social (organic) | paid-social (boosted)
  - utm_campaign: quick-win-social-2025-12
  - utm_content: testimonial | before-after | founder | offer | demo
  - (Optional) utm_term: audience slice (jobseekers, founders, freelancers)

- Daily check:
  - Sessions by UTM
  - CTR
  - Conversion rate

## Conversion Tracking Setup

- Meta: confirm pixel installed and firing a conversion event (Purchase or Checkout).
- LinkedIn: Insight Tag installed, conversion action configured.
- If no conversion tracking, use link clicks for 3 days, then add tracking ASAP.

## Asset Sourcing (Before/After)

- Create 2-3 internal examples using team photos or consenting users.
- Label as "example" if not from real customers.
- Avoid stock faces that imply actual user results.

## Policy / Trust Guardrails

- No claims like "guaranteed job" or "instant hiring."
- Avoid misleading before/after comparisons.
- Use only images with consent and clear usage rights.

## Community Management

- Reply to comments within 24 hours.
- Pin a helpful FAQ response on high-performing posts.
- DM interested users a short CTA + link.

## Posting Times (Local Timezone)

- LinkedIn: Tue-Thu 8-10am
- Instagram: Mon-Fri 11am or 7pm
- X: Mon-Fri 12pm or 5pm
- Facebook: Tue-Thu 1-3pm
