# Marketing Execution Tracker (Notion-ready)

## How to use
1. Create a new Notion database (table).
2. Add the columns below.
3. Paste the Seed Rows table to bootstrap tasks (or import as CSV).

## Columns
- Task ID (text)
- Task (title)
- Channel (select)
- Phase (select)
- Owner (person)
- Status (select)
- Priority (select)
- Due Date (date)
- KPI Target (text)
- KPI Result (text)
- Link (url)
- Notes (text)

## Suggested Select Values
- Channel: LinkedIn, Instagram, Meta Ads, X, Facebook, Google Search, Email, Website
- Phase: Phase 0, Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, Phase 4
- Status: Backlog, Ready, In Progress, Blocked, Done
- Priority: P0, P1, P2

## Week 1 Post Copy + Visual Notes

### LinkedIn Week 1 Copy
- **Mon – Transformation story**  
  Hook: “Before lunch: smeared lighting, after lunch: studio-ready headshot.”  
  Body: “We took a candid selfie, matched lighting with our studio filters, and delivered a polished LinkedIn photo in minutes. Recruiters now see confidence, not blur.”  
  CTA: “Book a 10-minute upload window.”  
  Visual: Carousel – left raw selfie, right polished headshot; overlay “Hire-ready in minutes.”
- **Tue – Checklist**  
  Hook: “5 photo mistakes recruiters notice instantly.”  
  Body: “Missing catchlights and uneven lighting cost you credibility. Upload 10 clear selfies, avoid filters, add variety in angles, follow our checklist, and we’ll do the rest.”  
  CTA: “Save the checklist + upload today.”  
  Visual: Vertical graphic listing the five checklist items with icons (lighting, angle, clarity, expression, background).
- **Wed – Process proof**  
  Hook: “Studio-quality headshot in 3 quick steps.”  
  Body: “Upload, we enhance, you approve. Every model gets layered proof cards showing how color cleanup, retouch, and framing combine to keep you looking authentic.”  
  CTA: “See the steps + send your selfies.”  
  Visual: 3-panel timeline showing “Upload → Review → Download” with subtle brand accent.
- **Thu – Speed proof**  
  Hook: “New headshot uploaded before afternoon meetings.”  
  Body: “Clients tell us they refresh their profile during lunch because the turnaround is that fast. We send proofs, you approve, and a polished photo rolls out in minutes.”  
  CTA: “Reserve your slot before 2 PM.”  
  Visual: Clock overlay with proof shots and the words “Fast + polished.”
- **Fri – CTA push**  
  Hook: “Need a new headshot before Monday interviews?”  
  Body: “We queue fast lanes for job seekers. Upload 10 selfies now, pick your favorite look, and share the polished headshot by Friday evening.”  
  CTA: “Get your headshot in minutes.”  
  Visual: Testimonial quote bubble + CTA button mockup referencing “10-minute upload window.”
