# SEO Editorial Rollout Guidelines (Remaining 16 Pages)

Use this checklist when updating the remaining SEO pages to the editorial proof pattern.

## Content Structure Order

1. Hero (max two CTAs)
2. Showcase/visual proof section (if images available)
3. Benefits/cards
4. Steps/process
5. FAQ
6. Related links
7. Final CTA band

## Hero Rules

- Keep hero copy concise: eyebrow + H1 + one short subhead.
- Include hero image when available (`imageSrc`, `imageAlt`) with 4:5 portrait crops.
- Use highlights for compact stat strip (2-4 entries).
- Keep CTAs to primary plus optional secondary only.

## Section Copy Rules

- Prefer short, concrete sentences.
- Card descriptions should be single-line to two-line max.
- Bullets should be action-oriented and scannable.
- Avoid repeated generic claims across sections.

## Visual Proof Rules

- Add `showcase` section as early as possible.
- Provide at least one before/after pair; two to three is preferred.
- Always set `beforeAlt` and `afterAlt` when images exist.
- Reuse existing `/assets/marketing/before-after/` sets until page-specific media is available.

## Design Consistency Rules

- Keep mobile-first ordering and spacing.
- Do not reintroduce glassmorphism, blur cards, or glow orbs.
- Keep CTA touch targets at least 48px tall.
- Do not add per-section enhancer CTAs.

## QA Checklist Per Page

- Verify no horizontal overflow at 320px and 375px.
- Verify showcase images stay contained and maintain aspect ratio.
- Verify exactly one H1 and expected metadata still render.
- Verify CTA count and placement match the editorial pattern.
- Verify readability in both light and dark themes.
