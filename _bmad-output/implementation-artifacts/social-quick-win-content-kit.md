1) Testimonial Card
- Headline: "Looks professional in minutes"

- CTA: "Try it now"

2) Promise Card
- Headline: "Studio-quality headshots. Low cost."
- Subhead: "Perfect for LinkedIn and applications"
- CTA: "Get your headshots"

3) Before/After (Use 2-up layout)
- Label: "Before" / "After"
- Caption: "Same person, upgraded for LinkedIn"

4) Offer/Speed Card
- Headline: "Headshots in minutes"
- Subhead: "No studio. No hassle."

### Story (1080x1920)
1) 2-frame demo
- Frame 1: "Upload a photo" + simple icon
- Frame 2: "Get a studio-quality headshot"

2) Testimonial story
- Frame 1: short quote
- Frame 2: CTA + link sticker

### Horizontal (1200x627)
1) LinkedIn/X promise
- Headline: "Upgrade your LinkedIn photo today"
- Subhead: "Studio-quality in minutes"

2) Before/after banner
- Left: Before | Right: After
- CTA: "See styles and pricing"

## Before/After Asset Guidance

- Use internal team photos or consenting users only.
- If examples are not real customers, label as "example".
- Avoid overly dramatic edits; keep realistic, professional results.
