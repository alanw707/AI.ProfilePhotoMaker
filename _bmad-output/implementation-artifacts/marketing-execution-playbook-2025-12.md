## Decision Lock (Week 1)
- **Core promise:** Studio-quality headshots in minutes at a fraction of the cost.
- **Primary CTA:** Get your headshot in minutes.
- **Default landing page:** `https://app.aiprofilephotomaker.com/pricing`
- **Campaign slug:** `social-quick-win-2025-12`
- **Primary persona:** Job seekers and professionals updating LinkedIn.
- **Secondary personas:** Freelancers, founders, remote workers.
- **Geo focus (Week 1):** Australia.
  - Expand to NZ/US/UK/CA in Week 2 if CPA is acceptable.
- **Paid budget (Week 1):** $10-$25/day for 7-14 days.
- **Paid split (Week 1):** LinkedIn 60%, Instagram 40%.
  - Rebalance on Day 7 based on CTR and purchase rate.

## Tracking Standards (Must Follow)
- **Base URL:** `https://app.aiprofilephotomaker.com/pricing`
- **UTM template:**
  - utm_source: linkedin | instagram | x | facebook
  - utm_medium: social (organic) | paid-social (boosted)
  - utm_campaign: social-quick-win-2025-12
  - utm_content: creative id (example: before-after-01, testimonial-amelia)
  - utm_term: optional persona tag (example: job-seeker)
- **Homepage test:** If CR is weak after Day 3, test homepage for 3 days.
  - Use `utm_content` suffix `-home`.

### Day 0 (Setup, 2-4 hours)
- Confirm pricing and CTA language on `/pricing`.
- Confirm testimonial permissions or anonymize.
- Lock creative template set (see kit below).
- Create UTM template links for each platform.
- Verify tracking status (GA4, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag).

### Days 1-3 (Baseline)
- Publish daily posts across all platforms.
- Boost 1 strong creative if pixel/Insight Tag is active.
- Log baseline CTR, checkout-start, purchase rate.

### Day 3 Decision Gate
- If purchase rate < 1% or checkout-start < 2%, test homepage vs `/pricing` for 3 days.

### Days 4-7 (Optimize)
- Continue cadence.
- Refresh copy on creatives with CTR < 1%.
- Ensure at least 3 testimonial-based posts.

### Day 7 Decision Gate
- Reallocate paid spend to top 2 creatives.
- Pause lowest performers.
- If IG CTR beats LinkedIn by 20%+, shift paid split to 60% IG / 30% LinkedIn / 10% FB/X.

### Days 8-14 (Scale winners)
- Keep daily cadence.
- Re-boost top 2 creatives.
- Maintain comment and DM response within 24 hours.

## Paid Boost Plan
- **Objective:** Conversions if tracking is active, otherwise Traffic.
- **Boost candidates:** Best testimonial, best before/after, best value prop.
- **Targeting:**
  - LinkedIn: job seekers, open to work, mid-career professionals.
  - Instagram: interests in LinkedIn, job search, career growth.

## Reporting Cadence
- **Daily:** clicks, CTR, checkout-start, purchase rate by creative id.
- **Weekly:** top 2 creatives, underperformers, update copy/creative.
- **Targets to start:** CTR >= 1%, checkout-start >= 3%, purchase >= 1.5%.

## Daily Runbook (5 minutes)
- Check sessions by utm_campaign and utm_content for the last 24 hours.
- Log CTR, checkout-start, purchase rate for each creative id.
- If CTR < 1% for any creative, refresh the first line of copy.
- If purchase rate < 1% after Day 3, switch to homepage test for 3 days.
- Reply to comments and DMs within 24 hours.
