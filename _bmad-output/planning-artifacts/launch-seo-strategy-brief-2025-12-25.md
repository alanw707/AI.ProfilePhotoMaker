# Launch SEO Strategy Brief: AI Profile Photo Maker

## Objectives (first 90 days)
- Establish an organic acquisition baseline with measurable weekly growth in impressions and clicks.
- Rank in the top 10 for 5-8 long-tail, high-intent queries within core clusters.
- Convert organic visitors at 3-5% to free enhancer usage and 1-3% to paid.
- Build trust signals that reduce perceived quality risk (examples, reviews, privacy).

## Primary ICP segments
- Job seekers and career switchers who need a credible LinkedIn profile fast.
- Professionals updating their LinkedIn presence for promotion or networking.
- Executives, founders, and client-facing roles with high brand sensitivity.

## Positioning statement
"LinkedIn-first, studio-quality headshots in minutes, with a free enhancer to prove the results before you pay."

## Differentiation anchors
- Free enhancer as a low-friction entry point (email capture + trust building).
- LinkedIn-first framing tied to credibility outcomes ("look the part").
- Clear quality proof: real before/after sets, consistent style, privacy emphasis.

## Funnel architecture
- TOFU: Free enhancer, educational content, "LinkedIn headshot" and "professional headshot" intent.
- MOFU: "AI headshot generator" and "headshot for job search" clusters.
- BOFU: Comparison pages (Aragon AI, HeadshotPro) and pricing/guarantee pages.
- Post-purchase: share prompts and referral incentives to drive word-of-mouth.

## SEO thesis
Focus on intent clusters where trust and outcomes matter, not just price. Build a tight internal link graph from cluster pages to the free enhancer and paid package CTA.

## Core pages (P0)
- Home
- Pricing
- Free enhancer
- How it works
- AI headshot generator
- LinkedIn headshots
- Professional headshots
- Headshots for job search/resume
- Comparison pages (Aragon AI vs, HeadshotPro vs)

## KPI targets
- Weekly: impressions, CTR, ranking movement for top 10 terms, enhancer starts.
- Monthly: organic sessions, enhancer-to-paid conversion, CAC (organic only).
- Quality: bounce rate by intent page, time on page, CTA engagement.

## Risks and mitigations
- Price parity and market crowding: differentiate on LinkedIn outcome and proof.
- Quality skepticism: publish transparent sample sets and style consistency FAQ.
- Over-claiming: keep all claims evidence-based and avoid unverifiable superlatives.

## Immediate next actions
- Publish P0 pages and 3 pillar pages with strong internal linking.
- Create 2 comparison pages using verified competitor claims.
- Add analytics goals for enhancer starts and paid conversion from SEO.
