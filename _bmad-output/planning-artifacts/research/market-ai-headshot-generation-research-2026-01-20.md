## Summary: The Path to Conversion

### What's NOT the Problem
- ✅ Your pricing is competitive
- ✅ Your product works
- ✅ You're getting traffic
- ✅ Your design is professional

### What IS the Problem
- ❌ Visitors don't immediately SEE the value (no before/after in hero)
- ❌ Visitors don't TRUST you (fake testimonials, no social proof numbers)
- ❌ Visitors don't feel URGENCY (no time promises, no guarantees visible)
- ❌ Visitors are CONFUSED (credit system vs headshot counts)

### The Fix (In Order)
1. **SHOW** value → Add before/after images to hero
2. **BUILD** trust → Remove fake testimonials, add real metrics, set up Trustpilot
3. **REDUCE** risk → Make money-back guarantee prominent
4. **SIMPLIFY** → Replace credit language with headshot counts
5. **OPTIMIZE** → Better headlines and CTAs
