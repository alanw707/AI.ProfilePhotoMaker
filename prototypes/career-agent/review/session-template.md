## Triage and retest

- **Launch-blocking:** a participant mistakes fictional occupational wages for a personal offer/pay prediction, cannot tell photos are optional, cannot review a proposed factual edit, or cannot complete a core task without instruction.
- **Material:** repeated hesitation, wrong market eligibility assumption, loss of saved work, focus/keyboard obstruction, or unclear source labels.
- **Minor:** cosmetic or wording preference without task impact.

For each material or launch-blocking finding, record the exact screen/state, participant count, proposed fix, change reference, and a retest with at least one participant who did not see the explanation.
