## Product and mode

Operate: an experienced U.S. professional must decide a next role, inspect evidence, choose a realistic market, and prepare work. Conversation stays near the task rather than becoming the only container.

## Visual concept

A personal research dossier. The surface feels like an annotated planning desk: clear section rules, light paper, compact source labels, dark ink and a deep teal mark for the selected or next action. The left rail is a table of contents, the main workspace is the current artifact, and the right panel is an assistant looking at the same evidence. On a phone, the artifact owns the screen and the assistant opens as a sheet.

The first viewport shows the current goal and next action. Tables and labeled evidence carry analysis; decorative scores and repeated salary cards do not. The source and uncertainty labels earn as much attention as a number.

## Behavior and content

- One highlighted navigation destination and one clear primary action per artifact.
- Labels identify fictional examples; no screenshot personal data or invented live source attribution.
- Comparable-pay range is unavailable until source qualification. The fictional occupational benchmark is labeled as a separate measure.
- Source, coverage, unknown, suppressed and stale are distinct states; the assistant explains the active artifact.
- Proposed edits require acceptance; saved work survives page navigation within the session.
- Map and table contain equivalent illustration, with the list first on mobile. Unknown cells do not share the low-value color.
- Photo assets are optional and separate from the default resume.
- At narrow widths the assistant occupies a sheet; Escape closes it and returns focus.
- Controls have visible focus, 44px targets where practical, reduced motion and non-color status wording.
